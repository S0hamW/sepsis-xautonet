    How It Works

    The system processes 19 clinical biomarkers collected during ICU telemetry. The 11 primary physiological indicators are standardized and passed through a 3-layer neural network trained on PhysioNet 2019 data. Sepsis risk is evaluated against an operational decision threshold of 0.30 to provide early warnings 6 hours ahead of potential onset.

    Research & About

    This application is an investigational research prototype for AI-assisted clinical decision support. Predictions represent statistical probability of sepsis onset within 6 hours based on retrospective ICU data. It is not validated for primary clinical diagnosis or independent medical intervention.
